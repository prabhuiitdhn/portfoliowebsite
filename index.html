I'm a Technical Specialist in Machine Learning at Continental Automotive (Bengaluru) with 8+ years of experience translating cutting-edge AI research into production systems. My work spans the full ML pipeline: from raw sensor data ingestion and synthetic dataset generation to model optimization and ASIL-D safety-certified hardware deployment.

Currently, I'm leading perception systems for autonomous vehicles, working with high-dimensional sensor data from IMX728/IMX623 cameras, multi-modal fusion (radar + camera), and edge AI inference on Ambarella CV3-AD SoCs. Beyond my industry work, I'm passionate about research in Vision-Language Models and Remote Sensing AI. I actively pursue advanced coursework (MIT MicroMasters in Statistics & Data Science, Stanford CS336: LLMs from Scratch) and read top-tier research papers to stay at the frontier of AI.
